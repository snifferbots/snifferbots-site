The product

Sensing hardware, smart software, and a shunt-trip that acts on its own.

Each SnifferBots unit lives inside a feeder compartment, reads the conditions around it, and works with a cloud application to warn you — or act — long before a fault becomes a fire.

The hardware, at a glance

One unit per feeder compartment.

Power input
110–230V AC, wide-band, surge protected
Onboard sensors
Temperature, humidity & gas — plus an ambient sensor probe
Connectivity
Wi-Fi to cloud, two-way, with fast auto-reconnect after power resumes
Switching
Onboard relay for shunt-trip, with inbuilt anti-pumping protection
Backup
Onboard data storage when offline · real-time clock with battery back-up
Updates
Over-the-air (OTA) programming
Alerts
Onboard buzzer for trip & connectivity intimations
Key points

Built as hardware you can trust, and software your team will actually use.

Hardware

Specially designed solid-state product. Works on wide-band 110–230V AC input, protected from surges.

Onboard sound buzzer for intimations about trips & connectivity issues.

Onboard real-time clock (RTC) with battery back-up for accurate, always-on timestamps.

Every unit connects an ambient temperature sensor; the system auto-detects it for dynamic protection algorithms.

Onboard relay for shunt-trip feeder tripping, with an inbuilt anti-pumping feature.

Hardware supports over-the-air programming for future updates.

Onboard storage keeps logging when a unit is powered but not connected — so you're never blind to a blank zone.

Built-in proactive trip logic: even with no internet or hub connectivity, hardware can self-generate a trip command in a critical situation.

Software

A purpose-built, web-based application designed around real UX & UI, not an afterthought.

Cloud-based — accessible, and controllable, from anywhere in the world.

Holds your entire electrical distribution system — projects, panels, feeders and authorised users — for future planning and emergency response.

Always-on live notifications keep you alert to feeders in warning, critical, or inactive zones — click straight through to the panel's full detail.

Authorised users can trip a feeder directly from the live notification screen, with command-execution confirmation.

Full feeder-wise trip history by user or system, date & time stamped — with room to log comments and experience for next time.

More capability is added continuously to keep the whole system reliable and genuinely intelligent for the user.

How does it work?

Simple, effective architecture — from feeder to phone.

1

Set up the client account

Projects, panels and feeders are entered into the SnifferBots web application, along with encrypted, authorised user access.

2

Install per feeder

Each SnifferBots unit — with onboard gas, humidity & temperature sensors — is installed inside its feeder compartment on a 230V AC, 50Hz supply.

3

Wire the shunt coil

The feeder's shunt coil is wired to SnifferBots, with anti-pumping built in to prevent contact chatter.

4

Connect to Wi-Fi

Units join your Wi-Fi network for two-way, cloud-connected communication.

5

Start monitoring

SnifferBots begins collecting and sending real-time data, so you can view the live status of every panel.

6

Analyse dynamically

The application's algorithms read and analyse feeder conditions with sensors working independently and in combination, judging the situation in advance.

7

Alert instantly

Situations reach your PC and mobile via email & SMS as a clear colour-coded signal. Authorised users can switch a feeder off directly from that alert.

8

Act on its own, if needed

If the feeder reaches a critical limit and nobody is reachable — no valid internet, a faulty PC, server, or phone — SnifferBots can generate its own proactive trip to stop the problem going further.

The alert system

One glance tells you exactly where you stand.

Sensor combinations are read continuously and translated into one of three plain-language signals — sent straight to PC and mobile by email and SMS.

Green — Normal

Conditions are healthy. No action needed.

Orange — Alarming

Something's drifting outside the healthy range. Time to take a look.

Red — Emergency

Critical limits reached. Trip the feeder remotely — or let SnifferBots do it for you.

Control is available from the user's mobile or PC to switch off a particular feeder in an extreme condition, whenever it's connected to the system over a valid internet connection.
