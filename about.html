Manufacturing facility

A 24,000 sq ft facility in Pune's industrial zone.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility sits in the designated Industrial Zone of Pune, Maharashtra, India, and is organised into two sections.

Built by the same team that designs SnifferBots — connected, IoT-driven manufacturing is in our own DNA, not just our product.

Manufacturing department

Houses all the machinery and manufacturing processes behind our electrical products.

Assembly, design & office

Mechanical and electrical assembly into complete final products — including switchboards, e-Comm parcel lockers, home pods and special enclosures — alongside a general & design office and an equipped conference room.

25 kW rooftop solar

Green power generated on our own factory rooftop, cutting our carbon footprint.

Sustainability

Building responsibly, alongside the trees we planted.

We run our factory in part on green power from a 25 kW solar plant installed on the rooftop — supporting our effort to cut down our carbon footprint. Around the facility, plenty of greenery and purposefully planted trees do the same.
